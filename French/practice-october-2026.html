                                  Les mots clés

                                    Tips from the sheet: days and months have no capital letters in French. Dates go “le + number + month” (le quatorze juillet), and the 1st is “le premier”. After an opinion verb you always need “the”: J’adore les mangas, J’aime le sport. To say you have none, use “je n’ai pas de …”, never “non” or “zéro”.

                                    The sheet spells “tiring” as “fatiguant”; the correct adjective is “fatigant”, which is what you’ll see and hear here.
